About

Who we are

Six decades of construction experience, from a rented room on Rizal Avenue to a Category AAA contractor shaping the country’s built future.

The company was established in 1965, engaging in General Building Construction under the name Archipelago Builders. The office started from renting a room of a building at Rizal Avenue, Sta. Cruz, Manila, with the warehouse and steel shop at Old Samson Road, Balintawak, Quezon City.

From Building Contractor–Category “C”, the firm rose to General Building Contractor–Class “B”, then in 1977 to General Engineering and General Building–Category “A”, engaged in both horizontal and vertical construction.

In 1979, Archipelago Builders built its own 3½-storey headquarters at 29 North Avenue, Diliman, Quezon City. Growth continued through Category “AA” in 1987 and Category “AAA” in 1998.

Today, after many years of construction experience, Archipelago Builders aims to further develop the quality of construction and help build the future for this country.
