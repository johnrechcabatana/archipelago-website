A story of competence and resilience

Built to serve the archipelago

Engr. Paulino Go Pe started Archipelago Builders in the mid-1960s with Php 500, determined to serve God and country by building across the islands.

Paulino was born in Manila and raised in Margosatubig, Zamboanga del Sur. He saw the hardships of his townmates during the Second World War. Those lessons shaped a modest, steady company. His dream was to build all over the archipelago — that is where the name comes from.

The first office was a rented room on Rizal Avenue in Sta. Cruz, Manila, with a warehouse and steel shop on Old Samson Road, Balintawak, Quezon City. One of the earliest jobs was a residence on D. Tuazon Street. In 1966 the firm built the Lirag Textile Mill in Montalban, Rizal. In 1981 it delivered the Mercury Drug head office and distribution center in Libis.

“It’s quite nice if we could construct all around the islands.”

Engr. Paulino Go Pe, Founder

Mission

Vision

Humble beginnings

From a rented room and a steel shop, the company took on housing and industrial work. About ten years in, personnel had doubled, three more rooms were rented, and the warehouse grew by three compartments. The license moved from PCAB Category C to Category B, opening the door to larger projects.

Category by category

The license was upgraded as the work grew. By 1987 Archipelago held a Double A category. In 1988 it earned Triple A as a general building contractor. The current PCAB regular contractor’s license, No. 33942, covers General Building and General Engineering at Category AAA, valid 4 September 2025 to 4 September 2027.

A house of its own

The head office on North Avenue rose in the 1980s. Through inflation, economic crisis, and political strife, the company was awarded work in Metro Manila, Southern Luzon, and the Visayas — factories, warehouses, hotels, and condominiums in Laguna, Bulacan, Rizal, Pangasinan, Batangas, Cavite, Benguet, Cagayan de Oro, Davao, Iloilo, Cebu, and Zamboanga.

Archipelago Builders Corporation

In March 2022 the sole proprietorship gave way to a new corporate citizen, Archipelago Builders Corporation, with the same aim: raise the quality of construction and help build the country’s future.

Room to take on more

Completed work stands at Php 25 billion, from industrial plants to high-rise condominiums. Five ongoing projects are takeovers that clients trusted ABC to finish. With current work considered, NFCC is still Php 9.6 billion, and the company is bidding or negotiating on 10 projects.

Resources

What ABC brings to site

Motorpool and tower cranes

Archipelago Builders keeps its own heavy and light equipment. The fleet includes 18 tower cranes. The equipment stockyard and motorpool sit in Angat, Bulacan, so plant can be staged close to Luzon sites.

Aluminum building system

Archipelago Builders was the first to use the aluminum building system in this market. It is an integrated forming and shoring system: lightweight forms for almost any application where monolithic reinforced cast-in-place concrete is specified. The formworks stockyard is in Angat, Bulacan.

Archiwall Corporation

Interior precast wall panels from Kaybitin Road corner C. Diaz St., San Gabriel, Santa Maria, Bulacan. Production is 300 panels in an 8-hour shift. A typical floor of 1,500 square meters is about 1,800 panels, which the plant can finish in a week.

archiwallcorp@gmail.com

Archicast

Exterior precast from the same Santa Maria plant, prepared so window openings are ready earlier and architectural work on the building can start sooner. Archicast shares the Kaybitin Road address with Archiwall.

Quality policy

The management of Archipelago Builders Corporation is dedicated to delivering construction projects that consistently exceed high quality standards across the organization. It does this by giving employees continuous education and training, and by putting customer needs first with professionalism and integrity.
